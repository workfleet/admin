# Accident records: appropriate policy document (draft)

DRAFT. This is a starting point, not legal advice. Have your health and
safety adviser or solicitor check it before you rely on it, then keep the
signed copy with your GDPR records.

The Data Protection Act 2018 (Schedule 1, Part 4) requires a document like
this when health data is processed under the employment condition. The
accident book in the app is processed under that condition.

## What we process

The accident, incident and near-miss reports in the app ("Accidents &
incidents" in the office menu), whether sent by staff or added by an admin. Each holds what happened, when and where, who was hurt, their name,
address and occupation, the injury, the part of the body, treatment, time
off work, witnesses, photos, and the office's follow-up, including the
RIDDOR category and HSE reference.

## Lawful basis and condition

- UK GDPR Article 6(1)(c), legal obligation:
  - Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1979, reg 25 (the accident book)
  - RIDDOR 2013 (reporting to HSE and keeping records)
  - Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 (investigating and reviewing risk)
- UK GDPR Article 9(2)(b), and DPA 2018 Schedule 1 paragraph 1: employment, social security and social protection.
- For members of the public and clients: Schedule 1 paragraph 1 still applies, because recording and reporting their injury is our duty as an employer and duty holder under RIDDOR.

## How we meet the data protection principles

| Principle | How the app meets it |
|---|---|
| Lawful, fair, transparent | Covered in the Privacy Notice in the app, under "Accidents, incidents and near misses". |
| Purpose limitation | Used only for the accident book, HSE reports, insurance claims, the client's site safety, and preventing a repeat. Never for performance management. |
| Data minimisation | The form asks only what the accident book and HSE's form need. The recorder's details are copied from their staff record, not typed in again. |
| Accuracy | What the reporter sends cannot be changed afterwards, by anyone (database trigger). The office adds findings beside it. A blank address or occupation can be filled in once. |
| Storage limitation | Kept at least 3 years from the accident (RIDDOR reg 12). Kept longer if a claim is open or possible, and until the injured person is 21 if they were under 18. The database refuses deletion before 3 years. Review the closed reports once a year and delete any past their period. |
| Security | Row-level security: only the reporter and admins can read a report. Supervisors cannot (0131). Photos are in a private storage bucket. All traffic is encrypted. |

## Who can see it

- The person who made the report: their own reports only.
- Admins: all reports.
- Supervisors: only the ones they sent themselves.
- HSE, our employer's liability insurer, and the client whose premises it happened on: only what each needs, sent by the office.
